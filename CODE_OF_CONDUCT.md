# Code of conduct

Help make Cave Paper welcoming to contributors of every background and skill
level. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and give
constructive feedback. Respect people's time, privacy, identity and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual misconduct, personal attacks,
publishing private information and repeated disruptive behavior are not
acceptable in project issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to info@cave.technology. Include links and the minimum
information needed to understand the incident; do not post private details
publicly. The maintainer will review reports and may remove content, restrict
participation or block accounts according to severity and repeated behavior.
Maintainers are expected to follow these standards too.
